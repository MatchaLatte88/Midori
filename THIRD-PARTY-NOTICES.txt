each package inside node_modules/ and are linked here by their canonical URL.

Midorii does not distribute market data. All price data is downloaded by the
user, on their own machine, from sources they choose, and remains subject to
those sources' own terms.

Fonts
-----
All fonts are self-hosted; Midorii makes no external font requests.

    Inter Tight — Rasmus Andersson
    SIL Open Font License 1.1
    https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/intertight/OFL.txt

    DM Mono — Colophon Foundry, Jonathan Pinhorn
    SIL Open Font License 1.1
    https://github.com/googlefonts/dm-mono/blob/main/OFL.txt

Inter and Plus Jakarta Sans were used until the move to the "Stille Karten"
design language. Their files are still in src/assets/fonts/ but no @font-face
rule references them, so they are not part of a build.
